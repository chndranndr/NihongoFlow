Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

NihongoFlow ("the App") is a Japanese language learning application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data Storage

All your learning data stays on your device. NihongoFlow stores progress, SRS card data, achievements, and preferences exclusively in your device's local storage. We do not operate servers and do not collect, transmit, or store your data remotely.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Advertising data

3. AI-Powered Features (Optional)

The App includes optional AI features (AI Grammar, AI Conversation, Image Analyzer) that use the Google Gemini API. These features:

  • Are disabled by default and only activated when you provide your own API key
  • Send text prompts or images directly from your device to Google's API — not through our servers
  • Are subject to Google's API Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy

We do not intercept, log, or store any data sent to or received from the Google Gemini API.

4. Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (or the Capacitor TTS plugin) for pronunciation. Text is processed locally on your device or by your device's default TTS provider. We do not send speech data to any third-party service.

5. Camera & Photo Access

The Image Analyzer feature may request access to your camera or photo library. Photos are:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Only sent to the Google Gemini API if you explicitly choose to analyze them (and have provided an API key)
  • Never stored, uploaded, or shared by the App

6. Internet Access

The App requires internet access only for:

  • Loading Google Fonts on first launch
  • AI-powered features (when enabled by you)

All drills, SRS reviews, grammar lessons, and gamification features work fully offline.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App complies with COPPA requirements.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by:

  • Clearing the App's data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the App

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
